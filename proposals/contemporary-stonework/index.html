An operations system, built in three phases.

From back-office order control to a native app in the field — a phased rollout that ships value early and compounds with every phase.

The three phases

Each phase ships a working surface your team uses before the next one begins.

Phase 1
  • Automatically checks every purchase order against what was delivered and flags discrepancies so revenue doesn't leak through the cracks.
  • Converts West Bay's plan files into usable documents automatically.
  • Pulls daily order data in from SupplyPro so the office isn't working across a dozen tabs.
  • Prepares dispute-protection emails, ready for one-click review and send.
  • Includes a feedback button from day one so the team shapes the tool as they use it.
Phase 2
  • Watches builder schedules automatically so the team stops working off stale dates.
  • Coordinates driver dispatch and routing from one place.
  • Gives Adrian a structured photo-audit workflow instead of hunting across platforms.
  • Gives Marcela one clean ordering workflow.
Phase 3
  • A native iOS app with a home-screen widget so the whole system is in his pocket.
  • GPS tracking for crews and drivers.
  • Push notifications so critical alerts reach him the moment they matter.
  • Published to the App Store.

A trilingual system — English in the office, Portuguese and Spanish in the field — runs through all three phases, so nothing gets lost in translation on the way from the desk to the jobsite.
