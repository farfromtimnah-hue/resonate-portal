Private · not for the screen

The gap

Everything valuable she produces — the profile, the colour analysis, the assembled looks, the before-and-afters, the written analysis — lives in her camera roll, her laptop files, and a WhatsApp thread. Clients refer back to it months later, from memory.

Her work is physical and visual. It can't survive as a PDF, and a link isn't something anyone keeps. Two routes, both viable: a Portuguese shell over the GoHighLevel account she already pays for, or something standalone. Be ready for either. Commit to neither.

Private · standing rules

What not to say

  • Don't pitch, quote, or propose. First conversation is understanding what she needs.
  • Never say her website looks templated. True, and the fastest way to lose her.
  • Don't tell her she has no system. She's on GoHighLevel.
  • Don't offer to build a DISC assessment. Licensed, and it doesn't survive translation. Storing results is fine.
  • Don't mention the weekend availability. A client praised it; reading it back as a problem is a diagnosis she didn't ask for.
  • Rafa is a bridge, not a channel. No pricing or problems through him unless she asks.
  • Her thesis: "O mercado não está pagando pouco, é o seu posicionamento que não comunica valor." She will hear a soft number as a self-diagnosis.
